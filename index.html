Happy Birthday, Mehnaz 🎂
from your brother, Abdul Ahad

Happy Birthday,

meri pyari behen. 🎂

Aaj ka din itna khaas hai

ke main isay yun hi guzarne

nahi de sakta tha

bina yeh bataye

ke aap meray liye kitni matter

karti hain.

Humein ek dusre ko jaante huay

bhale hi sirf kuch mahine

huay hain,

lekin sach mein aisa lagta hai

jaise aap saalon se

meri behen hain.

Kuch rishtay time se

nahi napay jatay,

wo is se napay jatay hain

ke koi banda apka dil ban k

kitni baar apke liye

khara hota hai,

aur aap har baar

khari rahi hain. 🌸

Yaad hai humari mulaqat

kaisay hui thi.

Bas do log jo WhatsApp pe

kaam ki wajah se

connect huay,

shuru mein sirf ek

professional baat cheet.

Lekin phir aap uss se

kahin zyada ban gayeen.

Shuru se hi jis tarah aap ne

meri care karna shuru kiya,

wo aisi cheez thi

jiski mujhe umeed nahi thi.

Yeh aisa nahi tha

ke aapko karna zaroori tha,

phir bhi aap ne

yeh sachche dil se kiya,

bina koi shor machaye,

sirf isliye ke aap

aisi hi hain.

Sach kahun to shayad

aapko khud bhi andaza nahi

ke aisi kindness

kitni rare hoti hai.

Log baatein karte hain

ek dusre ke sath

khara hone ki,

lekin aap ne wo waqai kiya,

khamoshi se, sacchay dil se,

jaisay yeh ek behen ke liye

sab se normal si baat ho.

Aur yehi aap meray liye

ban gayi hain,

sirf naam ki muu boli

sis nahi,

balkay har maine mein

ek asli behen. 🦋

Meri dua hai

ke yeh birthday aapko

utni hi khushiyan de

jitni aap ne dusron ko

di hain,

balkay us se bhi zyada.

Allah aapko sehat,

sukoon, kamyabi,

aur aise logon se nawaze

jo aapki utni hi care karein

jitni aap sab ki karti hain.

Har wo dua qubool ho

jo aap ne kabhi

khamoshi se maangi ho,

aur zindagi ka yeh naya saal

kam stress, zyada hansi,

aur achi khabron se

bhara ho. ✨

Shukriya ke aap

aisi behen hain

jo logon ki care karti hain

bina kuch wapis maange.

Shukriya support k liye,

aur ek work connection ko

asli rishtay mein

badalnay k liye.

Umeed hai main bhi kabhi

aapke liye waisay hi

khara ho sakun

jaisay aap meray liye

khari rahi hain.

Ek baar phir

Happy Birthday, Mehnaz.

Blessed rahiye,

caring rahiye,

aur bilkul waisi hi

rahiye jaisi hain,

kyunke duniya ko

aap jaisay logon ki

zaroorat hai. 🎀

Dil se,
Aapka bhai,
Abdul Ahad 🎀
